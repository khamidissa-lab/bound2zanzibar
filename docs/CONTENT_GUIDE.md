# Content Guide

## Brand name
BOUND TO ZANZIBAR

## Suggested tagline
Your Journey Begins in Zanzibar.

## Brand voice
- Warm and welcoming
- Clear and practical
- Locally knowledgeable
- Exciting without exaggeration
- Trustworthy and transparent about what is included

## Sample homepage headline
Discover Zanzibar, Your Way.

## Sample supporting copy
From historic Stone Town and turquoise-water adventures to cultural experiences and unforgettable Tanzania safaris, discover journeys designed around the way you want to travel.

## Audience-sensitive content
- Residents: emphasize accessible local experiences, clear TZS pricing and group options.
- East African travellers: emphasize flexible trips, practical transport and regional travel.
- International visitors: explain logistics, inclusions, meeting points and booking steps clearly.

## Content accuracy rules
- Do not claim a tour is available every day unless verified.
- Do not claim a hotel, boat, vehicle or guide is company-owned unless confirmed.
- Do not invent customer reviews or partner logos.
- Use owner-approved prices and cancellation policies before launch.
- Clearly state whether meals, transfers, park fees and equipment are included.
- Avoid guarantees about wildlife sightings or dolphin encounters.
